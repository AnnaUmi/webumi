PWA or app store?

Two ways to build an app.

Both work on iPhone and Android. The difference is how people get the app and what it can do.

Web app (PWA)

A progressive web app opens from a link and can be added to the home screen, where it looks and works like an app.

  • No app store approval or fees
  • One link for everyone: website, QR code, Instagram
  • Logins, bookings, payments and push notifications
  • Updates go live instantly
  • Lower cost to build and run

Best for: customer portals, rebooking and loyalty apps, staff and job apps.

iOS & Android app

A native-feeling app published in the Apple App Store and Google Play, built from one codebase for both.

  • Found by searching the app stores
  • Full access to phone features
  • The most familiar experience for users
  • Store review before each release
  • Yearly Apple fee and one-off Google fee

Best for: apps people search for, and apps that need camera, location or background features.

Not sure? Most small businesses start with a PWA. It can become an app store app later without starting from scratch.

App questions

Common questions about apps.

What's a PWA?

A progressive web app (PWA) is an app that runs from a web link. People add it to their home screen, and it opens full screen like a normal app, including on iPhone. It can send notifications and work with logins and payments, without going through an app store.

How much does it cost to build an app in Australia?

With Webumi, a web app (PWA) starts at $8,900 and an iOS & Android app starts at $14,900. Australian agencies commonly quote $20,000 or more for a basic app. The final price depends on the number of screens and features.

Do I need to pay Apple and Google?

Only for app store apps. Apple charges a yearly developer membership and Google Play charges a one-off registration fee, both paid directly by you so the app is published under your business. A PWA doesn't need either.

Who owns the app?

You do. The code, the app store listings and the accounts are yours.

What does it cost to keep an app running?

Apps need hosting and regular updates as phones change. We'll quote a support plan with the project so you know the ongoing cost upfront.
