    Not sure which one?

    A quick guide.

    • Choose a landing page if…

      You're just starting, you offer one main service, or you need a page for an ad campaign. One page, one clear action.

    • Choose a business website if…

      You want to be found on Google for several services or areas, and you want customers to enquire or book online.

    • Choose an app if…

      Customers or staff use it again and again: rebooking, loyalty, orders or job tracking. It lives on their home screen.

    • Choose a platform if…

      People need to log in and manage things: a client portal, a dashboard, a marketplace or your own software product.

    Pricing questions

    About quotes and payments.

    How much does a small business website cost in Australia?

    With Webumi, a landing page starts at $590 and a complete business website starts at $1,290 (Essential design), or $1,190 and $2,490 with a Signature design. For comparison, Australian agencies commonly charge $3,000 to $8,000 for a standard small business website.

    What's the difference between Essential and Signature?

    Essential uses a proven layout set up with your logo, colours and fonts, with fade-in and hover animations. Signature is designed for your brand: you approve a mockup first, and it includes an animated video hero, up to 10 sections with scroll effects, sliders or carousels, up to 4 video sections and custom icons. Both load fast, work on every screen size and get the same Google setup. Signature costs $600 more for a landing page, $1,200 more for a website and $2,900 more for an app.

    How much does an app cost?

    A web app (PWA) starts at $8,900 and an iOS & Android app starts at $14,900. The final price depends on the number of screens and features, such as logins, payments and notifications.

    Why fixed prices instead of hourly rates?

    So you know the full cost before you commit. If we underestimate the work, that's on us, not you.

    How do payments work?

    Usually 50% to start and 50% at launch. Larger apps and platforms are split into stages, paid as each stage is delivered. Everything is invoiced under our ABN.

    What if I only need something small?

    Small jobs are welcome, like fixing a contact form or speeding up a slow website. Tell us what you need and we'll quote it.
