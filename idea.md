Yes — absolutely. As an Australian sole trader with an ABN, you can sell website development, app development, automation, digital setup, consulting, UI/UX and similar services to Australian businesses. You do not need to create a company/Pty Ltd just to start. ASIC describes sole trader as the simplest business structure: one person owns and controls the business. 

You can also have a normal salaried job and separately earn business income as a sole trader. Your sole-trader profit is reported through your individual tax return. 

For what you're proposing, I'd keep the setup very simple:

ABN: you already have this.
Business name: if your website operates under a brand such as “Nexa Digital” rather than your personal first name + surname, register that business name with ASIC. 
Invoices: invoice clients using your ABN. 
GST: if your GST turnover reaches or is expected to reach $75,000 over a 12-month period, you generally need to register for GST. Below that, registration is usually optional. 
Contract: have a simple service agreement defining scope, price, deposit, revisions, ownership of the finished work, cancellation and what happens when clients delay providing content.
Insurance: professional indemnity insurance is worth considering once you start taking larger clients, although that's different from needing a special licence to build websites.
So you could literally start with:

Anna [Surname] — Sole Trader
ABN XX XXX XXX XXX
trading as [Your Brand]

and accept a $2,000–$5,000 website project.
