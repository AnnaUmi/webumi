Mew&Co

A landing page concept for a cat-food brand. The cat in the video watches the visitor's cursor: the closer it gets to the "feed me!" button, the more the cat turns to look, and the headline changes with it. Press the button and dinner drops in.

  • Video that follows the cursor, frame by frame
  • Headline that changes as the cursor gets closer
  • Food drops when the button is pressed
  • On phones, a tap plays the ending
  • Fits any screen shape without cropping the cat

MyTalisman

A daily practice app that turns a personal goal into a one-of-a-kind emblem. Users answer a few questions, the app forges their sign, and a one-minute daily ritual (breath, a spoken affirmation and an if-then plan) keeps the goal in front of them.

  • Your sign as a lock-screen wallpaper
  • Voice affirmations recorded and kept only on the phone
  • Free trial and in-app subscriptions

Wealth Frequency

A daily money-mindset app. Each morning, a one-minute ritual in five steps (affirm, reflect, embody, gratitude, lock in) along a 365-day journey. Every affirmation is written by AI for that person, based on their answers when they sign up.

  • A 365-day journey in six cycles
  • A virtual wealth balance styled like a banking app

  • Progress score with streaks and milestones, in 150+ currencies
