Aellita

The website of Aellita, a decorator studio in Prague 2 that has made curtains for flats, villas and historic palaces for twenty years. It opens on a bare room with a view of Prague Castle, and as you scroll the window is dressed step by step: measuring, a sheer, velvet curtains, cushions, evening.

  • A room that is dressed as you scroll, with a label for each fabric
  • A daylight meter that drops as the curtains close
  • A real clip for each service: motorised curtains, blinds, wallpaper, reupholstery
  • A velvet pincushion that squishes when you poke it
  • In Czech, with a free measuring visit booked from the page

T1ER ONE Activewear

AI video and a one-page concept site for an Australian women's activewear brand. Every athlete wears the brand's own crop tee and shorts, with the real logos where the garment mock-ups put them. The page opens by flying through the red 1 of the logo.

  • AI athletes training in the client's garments: back squat, kettlebell swing, a walk from office to gym
  • The brand's real logos on chest, sleeves and waistband, checked frame by frame
  • Each athlete cut out of her clip and lit by the cursor; a click shows her in full colour
  • A line that follows the cursor splits her office from her gym
  • The shorts change colour on the photo itself

Square & Plumb

A website concept for a Sydney furniture assembly business. It opens on an empty wall. As you scroll, a laser level marks it out and a kitchen goes in piece by piece, while a job clock counts the days on site.

  • Each piece slides, drops or rolls into place as you scroll, forwards and back
  • Laser level lines, landing zones and a tag for every cabinet
  • On phones, the camera follows the work across the room
  • One click on “Scroll to build” plays the whole build
  • Before-and-after slider and a quote form that takes photos of the boxes

Mew&Co

A landing page concept for a cat-food brand. The cat in the video watches the visitor's cursor: the closer it gets to the "feed me!" button, the more the cat turns to look, and the headline changes with it. Press the button and dinner drops in.

  • Video that follows the cursor, frame by frame
  • Headline that changes as the cursor gets closer
  • Food drops when the button is pressed
  • On phones, a tap plays the ending
  • Fits any screen shape without cropping the cat

MyTalisman

A daily practice app that turns a personal goal into a one-of-a-kind emblem. Users answer a few questions, the app forges their sign, and a one-minute daily ritual (breath, a spoken affirmation and an if-then plan) keeps the goal in front of them.

  • Emblems generated from each person's answers
  • Your sign as a lock-screen wallpaper
  • Voice affirmations recorded and kept only on the phone
  • Free trial and in-app subscriptions
