What I do

Modern web skills, for businesses that don't have a tech team.

I build web products professionally: websites, web apps, payment and booking integrations, and AI features. Webumi brings those skills to small businesses that need them but don't have a developer on staff.

Most of my work falls into four areas: websites, online bookings, automation and AI assistants. Often it's a mix, starting small and adding more as the business grows.

How I work

Build what helps, skip what doesn't.

Most small businesses don't need custom software. They need tools that already exist, set up properly and connected together. That's usually faster, cheaper and easier for you to run.

When something custom is genuinely the better option, such as an AI assistant, a client portal or an internal tool, I build that too, and I'll explain why before you pay for it.
