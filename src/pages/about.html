What we do

Modern web skills, for businesses that don't have a tech team.

We build web products professionally: websites, web apps, payment and booking integrations, and AI features. Webumi brings those skills to small and mid-sized businesses that need them but don't have a developer on staff.

Most of our work falls into four areas: websites, online bookings, automation and AI assistants. Often it's a mix, starting small and adding more as the business grows.

How we work

Build what helps, skip what doesn't.

Most small businesses don't need custom software. They need tools that already exist, set up properly and connected together. That's usually faster, cheaper and easier for you to run.

When something custom is genuinely the better option, such as an AI assistant, a client portal or an internal tool, we build that too, and we'll explain why before you pay for it.

Our name

A little bear and a guiding star.

The "UMi" in Webumi comes from Ursa Minor, the Little Bear constellation. Astronomers write it as UMi. Its brightest star is Polaris, the North Star, which sailors used for centuries to find their way.

Those two ideas are how we work. The bear stands for character: every business has its own, and its website should show it. The star stands for direction: we help you choose what your website needs and leave out what it doesn't.

You'll see both in our logo, where the star is the dot on the "i" and the only part that moves.
