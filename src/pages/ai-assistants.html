Set up carefully

What it won't do.

A badly set up chatbot is worse than none. Yours is built with clear limits:

  • It only answers from information you've approved: your services, prices, policies and FAQs.
  • It hands over when it isn't sure, instead of guessing.
  • Customers can always reach you directly. It never hides your phone or email.
  • It doesn't replace you for quotes that need a site visit or a conversation.

Before launch we test it together with the real questions your customers ask, and adjust anything that doesn't sound like you.

AI questions

Common questions about AI assistants.

Is an AI assistant safe to use with my customers?

Yes, when it's set up carefully. It only uses information you approve, runs on established AI providers, and hands over to you whenever it isn't sure. We'll explain what data it sees and how it's stored, so you stay on the right side of Australian privacy rules.

Which AI does it use?

It's built on well-known AI models, such as those from OpenAI, Anthropic or Google, chosen for the job. You don't need your own accounts to start; we'll explain the options.

What does it cost to run each month?

AI providers charge by usage, which for most small businesses is modest. We'll estimate your monthly running cost from your enquiry volume before we start.

Will customers know they're talking to AI?

Yes. It introduces itself as an assistant, and customers can ask for a person at any time. Being upfront builds more trust than pretending.

Can it answer phone calls?

Voice assistants are possible for some businesses. Tell us how your calls work and we'll tell you honestly whether it's a good fit.
