Behind it, Calendly or a similar tool handles calls, Stripe handles payments, and simple automations send the intake form, reminders and follow-ups.

Coach & tutor questions

Common questions from coaches and tutors.

Can clients pay for a package of sessions upfront?

Yes. Clients can buy a package online by card, and you can offer payment plans for larger programs.

Can I run sessions over Zoom or Google Meet?

Yes. Booking tools like Calendly can create a video link for each session automatically and include it in the confirmation and reminders.

Can I sell an online course?

For simple course or resource sales, yes. For a full course platform, we'll recommend a dedicated tool that fits and connect it to your website.
