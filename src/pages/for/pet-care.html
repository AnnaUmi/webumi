Pet business questions

Common questions from pet businesses.

Can I charge different prices by dog size?

Yes. Services can be priced by size or coat type, and the booking form shows the right price for each pet.

Does this work for mobile groomers?

Yes. Bookings include the address, and you can limit which areas you visit on which days to keep your runs efficient.

Can owners book several pets at once?

Yes, with most booking systems. Each pet gets its own details and service, in one booking.
