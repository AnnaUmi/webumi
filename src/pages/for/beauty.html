Salon questions

Common questions from salons and studios.

I already use Fresha. Can you work with it?

Yes. We'll connect your website, Instagram and Google profile to your existing Fresha booking page, and add anything it doesn't do for you.

Can clients choose a specific stylist or therapist?

Yes. Most booking systems let clients pick a team member, and only show that person's available times.

Can I take deposits for some services only?

Yes. You can require deposits for long or high-value services, such as colour or extensions, and not for quick ones.
