Cleaning business questions

Common questions from cleaners.

Can my website give customers an instant price?

Yes. We build a quote calculator from your own prices, covering bedrooms, bathrooms, type of clean and extras, so the price shown is the price you'd quote.

Can customers book regular weekly or fortnightly cleans?

Yes. Recurring bookings repeat automatically, with a reminder before each clean and an easy way to skip or reschedule one.

Can bookings go straight to my staff?

Yes. Jobs can be assigned to a cleaner with the address, checklist and access notes, and appear in their calendar.
