Instant quotes

Your rate card, working 24/7.

Most cleaning prices follow clear rules: bedrooms, bathrooms, type of clean and extras. We turn your rate card into a simple calculator on your website, so customers see an accurate price straight away and can book it on the spot.

For jobs that don't fit the rules, like large commercial sites, the form becomes a quote request with photos, and you reply personally.

Cleaning business questions

Common questions from cleaners.

Can my website give customers an instant price?

Yes. We build a quote calculator from your own prices, covering bedrooms, bathrooms, type of clean and extras, so the price shown is the price you'd quote.

Can customers book regular weekly or fortnightly cleans?

Yes. Recurring bookings repeat automatically, with a reminder before each clean and an easy way to skip or reschedule one.

Can bookings go straight to my staff?

Yes. Jobs can be assigned to a cleaner with the address, checklist and access notes, and appear in their calendar.

Do I need a separate page for end-of-lease cleaning?

Yes, if you want to be found for it. People search for "end of lease cleaning" separately from "house cleaning", and a page with its own checklist and price is more likely to rank than a line on your home page.
