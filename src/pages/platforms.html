Start small

Why every platform starts with an MVP.

An MVP (minimum viable product) is the smallest version that does the most important job properly. Instead of spending months building every feature you can imagine, you launch the core, get it into real users' hands, and then build what they actually use.

It keeps the first investment smaller, gets you results sooner, and means later features are based on real feedback instead of guesses.

Before any code is written, you get a written plan: what the first version does, what comes later, the fixed price and the timeline.
