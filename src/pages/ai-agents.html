    Questions

    About AI agents.

    What's the difference between an AI assistant and an AI agent?

    An AI assistant answers questions, like a front desk. An AI agent has a specific job: it asks the questions it needs and produces a finished result, such as a quote, a plan or a report.

    Can you add AI to my current website?

    Yes. A chat assistant or an agent can be added to WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Shopify or a custom site without rebuilding it.

    How much does the AI itself cost to run?

    The AI provider charges per use, usually cents per conversation. Every agent gets a daily spending cap, so the cost can't run away.

    Will the AI make things up?

    Agents are limited to your own information. Prices always come from your price list and are calculated by the page, not written by the AI.

    What happens to customers' answers?

    Conversations are sent to the AI provider to generate replies, and the result is sent to you. We'll explain exactly what's stored and where before we launch, and add it to your privacy page.
