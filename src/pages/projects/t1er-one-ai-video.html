Case study · AI video · Activewear

AI athletes in T1ER ONE's own activewear.

T1ER ONE is an Australian activewear brand getting ready for its women's drop: the Power Crop Tee and the Seamless Sculpt Short. They asked us whether AI video could show their real garments and logos, not generic sportswear. We made a test clip, four more clips for two campaigns, and a concept website to show them in.

The brief

Their garments, their logos, realistic movement.

The client sent us mock-ups of the crop tee and the shorts, and the women's emblem: an athlete kneeling with her arms out, a red 1 and the T1ER ONE wordmark. The brand has two campaign lines:

  • Unlock the Power: the strength and confidence you already have
  • Unlock the Beast: discipline, drive and one more rep

For the free test clip they asked for a women's office-to-gym scene, with the emblem placed exactly as on the mock-up.

Two logos in the brief

One emblem on the tee, another on the shorts.

The brand's materials carry two versions of the emblem. The tee mock-up uses a muscular figure; the shorts and the emblem file use the women's figure. We followed each mock-up as it was, so both appear in the clips, and the client picks one for the final ads.

The test clip

Office out. Gym in.

She pulls off her blazer in the office corridor, and the scene cuts to her standing in the gym in the T1ER ONE kit. It's the scene the client asked for, with the logos on the chest, sleeves and shorts where the mock-ups put them.

  • The same face and figure in both scenes
  • Logos placed from the client's mock-ups
  • Vertical, for Reels, TikTok and Stories

The campaign clips

Three athletes, cut out and ready for any background.

We made each clip on a green screen, cut the athlete out and lit her in the brand's black and red. The same cut-outs run on the concept site, where the cursor lights them.

The opening film

The emblem pose, brought to life.

For the top of the concept site, we turned the emblem's pose into a film: arms out, fists closed, red haze behind her and the emblem on her chest. The page shows it through the letters of T1ER ONE, then flies through the red 1 as you scroll.
