      Good questions

      Things people usually ask.

      Do I need to be good with technology?

      No. Everything is set up so you can run it from your phone, and you get a short video walkthrough at handover.

      How long does it take?

      A website is usually 2–3 weeks. Bookings and automations add a week or two. The biggest factor is how quickly you can send me photos and info — I'll give you a simple checklist.

      Do I need to pay monthly for anything?

      Some tools have their own fees (for example, a booking system or domain), and I'll tell you exactly which ones and what they cost before we start. Many have free plans. My hosting & support plan is optional.

      I already use Square / Fresha / Xero. Do I have to switch?

      No. I'd much rather connect what you already use than make you learn something new.

      Is AI safe to use with my customers?

      It is when it's set up carefully. Your assistant only uses the information you approve, it's built on established AI providers, and it hands over to you whenever it isn't sure. I'll walk you through what data it sees and how it's stored, so you stay on the right side of Australian privacy rules.

      Will an AI chatbot annoy my customers?

      Not when it's set up well. It answers quickly and in your tone, and customers can always reach you directly. It handles after-hours questions so you don't miss enquiries.

      How do payments work?

      Usually 50% to start and 50% at launch. Everything is invoiced properly under my ABN.

      What if I just want one small thing fixed?

      Yes. Small jobs are welcome, like fixing a contact form or speeding up a slow website.
