      How it works

      From your ideas to your live website.

      1. 1

        Tell us about your business.

        Complete a guided form about your business, customers and goals. Share any branding, photos or text you already have. We'll identify anything else your project needs.

      2. 2

        Get your fixed quote.

        We send a quote listing the pages and features included, one fixed price and the timeline. Once you're happy, sign the agreement online and pay the 50% deposit.

      3. 3

        Three directions. Your choice.

        Choose the look that feels like your business.

        Explore three homepage design concepts created around your brand and goals. Choose your favourite, then we refine it together before designing the rest of your website.

      4. 4

        Approve your website plan.

        A visual sitemap shows each page and what it will include. For a landing page, it shows each section. You check the plan and approve it.

      5. 5

        Review your website's design and copy.

        We design the remaining pages in your chosen style and write or polish the text from what you've shared. You review both and approve them before we build.

      6. 6

        Preview your website before launch.

        Explore your website on your phone and computer, and share your final feedback. We check the layouts, forms, links and interactions before you approve the launch.

      7. 7

        Go live on your domain.

        We connect your domain, publish your website and complete the final checks. For the first 30 days, we fix any issues with the agreed functionality at no extra cost.
