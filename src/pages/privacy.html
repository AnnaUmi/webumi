Privacy

Privacy policy.

What we collect on webumi.com.au, why we collect it and what we do with it. Webumi, ABN 42 183 713 603, Sydney NSW. We follow the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

Details you send us. When you use the guided order form, the contact form, the call booking form, the non-profit application or the website planner, we receive what you type in: usually your name, email, phone number, business name and details about your project, plus any files you attach.

A bot check. Our forms use Cloudflare Turnstile to block spam. It checks your browser and doesn't ask you to solve puzzles.

Usage limits on the planner. To keep the AI planner from being overused, we store a one-way code made from your IP address together with a count of messages. We can't turn that code back into your IP address.

No tracking. This website has no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. A plan made in the planner is stored in its own link, not in an account.

Why we collect it

To reply to your message, prepare your quote or plan, arrange the call you asked for and deliver your project. We don't sell your details, and we don't add you to a mailing list unless you ask us to.

Who processes it

Hostinger hosts this website and our email. Form messages are emailed to us, and a copy is kept in a private folder on our hosting that isn't reachable from the web.

OpenAI processes the messages you type into the website planner, so the AI can ask questions and write your plan. Don't type passwords, payment details or anything confidential into the planner.

Cloudflare runs the bot check on our forms.

Some of these services store data outside Australia. We only share what each one needs to do its job.

How long we keep it

As long as we need it to answer you and deliver your project, and as long as Australian law requires for business and tax records. Ask us and we'll delete what we don't have to keep.

Your choices

You can ask to see the details we hold about you, correct them or have them deleted. Email {{email}} and we'll reply within 5 business days.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your details, tell us first so we can fix it. You can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
