The common problems

Why many small business websites don't bring in work.

Most of the websites we review look fine. They just don't help a customer take the next step. Here's what goes wrong, and what we do instead.

  • The headline doesn't say what you do or where.

    Your service and suburb in the first line, so visitors know in seconds they're in the right place.

  • No clear next step on a phone.

    A call button and a short quote form that stay one tap away on every page.

  • One page for everything.

    A page for each service and area, so Google can match you to "blocked drain Parramatta".

  • No reviews or photos of real work.

    Your Google reviews and project photos placed right where people decide.

  • Slow loading.

    No heavy templates, and images and video compressed, so pages open fast even on mobile data.

  • Enquiries that go nowhere.

    Forms that reach your phone, and an instant reply so the customer doesn't call someone else.

Website questions

Before you get started.

Do you build online shops?

Yes. We add an online shop to your website from {{aud:store:website}}: up to 20 products, cart and checkout, shipping rules and order emails, on Shopify or Stripe. Larger shops are quoted once we've discussed your range.

What's the difference between a landing page and a business website?

A landing page is one page focused on a single action, like calling or booking. A business website has several pages, one for each service and area, which helps you show up in more Google searches.

How long does a website take?

A landing page usually takes 3 days – 1 week and a business website 1–2 weeks, from when we receive your photos and details. We'll give you a short checklist of what to send.

Do I have to write all the text?

No. You tell us about your services and customers, and we write the website text for you to check and approve.

Will my website show up on Google?

We set up everything Google needs to understand and index your site, including your Google Business Profile. Where you rank also depends on reviews, competition and how long the site has been live, so nobody can honestly guarantee the top spot.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes, with the simple content editor ({{aud:cms-simple:landing}} for a landing page, {{aud:cms-simple:website}} for a website) you can change text, photos, prices and opening hours yourself. Or email us the changes with a support plan from {{aud:care:monthly}}/month, which includes monthly updates.

Who owns the website and domain?

You do. The domain, hosting and accounts are set up in your name, and you can take them anywhere.
