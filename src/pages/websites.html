The common problems

Why many small business websites don't bring in work.

Most of the websites we review look fine. The problem is that they don't help a customer take the next step. The usual causes:

  • The headline doesn't say what you do or where. Visitors leave within seconds if they can't tell.
  • No clear next step on mobile. The phone number is hidden or the contact form is at the bottom of a long page.
  • One page for everything. Google can't match you to "blocked drain Parramatta" if that service has no page.
  • No reviews or photos of real work. People trust other customers more than your own description.
  • Slow loading. Heavy templates and huge images lose visitors before the page appears.
  • Enquiries that go nowhere. Forms that land in spam, or replies that take a day.

We build around these from the start, so the website does its job: more enquiries.

Website questions

Before you get started.

What's the difference between a landing page and a business website?

A landing page is one page focused on a single action, like calling or booking. A business website has several pages, one for each service and area, which helps you show up in more Google searches.

How long does a website take?

A landing page usually takes 1–2 weeks and a business website 2–4 weeks, from when we receive your photos and details. We'll give you a short checklist of what to send.

Do I have to write all the text?

No. You tell us about your services and customers, and we write the website text for you to check and approve.

Will my website show up on Google?

We set up everything Google needs to understand and index your site, including your Google Business Profile. Where you rank also depends on reviews, competition and how long the site has been live, so nobody can honestly guarantee the top spot.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes, with the simple content editor ({{aud:cms-simple:landing}} for a landing page, {{aud:cms-simple:website}} for a website) you can change text, photos, prices and opening hours yourself. Or email us the changes: free for {{price:support:freeDays}} days after launch, then included in a support plan from {{aud:care:monthly}}/month.

Who owns the website and domain?

You do. The domain, hosting and accounts are set up in your name, and you can take them anywhere.
