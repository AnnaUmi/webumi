Can AI photos show my real product?

Yes. Send a few clear phone photos of the product and we place it in new scenes: on a kitchen bench, in a gift box, on a model or in a styled flat lay. We check every image so the product looks the way it does in real life.

Can I use the videos and images in paid ads?

Yes. You own the finished files and can use them anywhere, including Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and Google ads.

Do AI ads need to be labelled?

Facebook, Instagram and TikTok ask advertisers to label ads with realistic AI-made people or scenes. Illustrated and cartoon styles, like our bear, usually don't need a label. We tell you when it applies.

What do you need from me?

What the video or images are for, where they will run, your logo and colours, and photos of your product or team if they should appear. Examples of ads or styles you like help too.

Can my brand character appear in my videos later?

Yes. That's what the character sheet is for: the same mascot can star in every future video ad, post and website section.
