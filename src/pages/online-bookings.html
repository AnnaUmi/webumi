Why it matters

Every booking by message costs you time.

"How much is it?", "Do you have anything Thursday?", "Can I move to 3pm?" Each booking by DM or phone takes several messages, often while you're with another client.

With online booking, customers see your prices and real availability and book themselves, day or night. A deposit makes them far more likely to turn up, and automatic reminders handle the rest.

You don't need to learn new software from scratch. I set everything up, connect it to your website, Google profile and Instagram, and show you how to manage it from your phone.

Booking questions

Common questions about online booking.

Do I have to change the booking software I use now?

Usually not. If your current system works, I'll connect it to your website and add deposits, reminders and review requests around it.

Can customers book from Instagram and Google?

Yes. Your booking link goes in your Instagram bio and on your Google Business Profile, so people can book from wherever they find you.

What happens when a customer cancels?

You choose the rules: how much notice is needed, and whether the deposit is refunded, kept or moved to a new booking. The system applies them automatically.

Are there monthly fees?

Some booking systems charge monthly or per booking, and SMS reminders have small per-message costs. I'll show you the options and costs before we choose one.
