Non-profits

Free website help for volunteer-run non-profits.

Webumi supports Australian non-profits and community groups that run on volunteers. If a website or an online tool would help your cause, tell us about it. If we can help, the work is free.

What we can help with

The online tools volunteers need most.

  • {{icon:doc}}

    A simple website

    A landing page or small website that explains what you do and how people can help or get help.

  • {{icon:wrench}}

    Fixing your current site

    Broken forms, outdated pages, a slow site or one that doesn't work on phones.

    Volunteer sign-ups

  • {{icon:calendar}}

    Event registrations

  • {{icon:card}}

    Online donations

    A donate button connected to Stripe or PayPal, with a thank-you email.

  • {{icon:sparkle}}

Who it's for

Community groups doing good work.

  • Australian non-profits, charities and community groups
  • Run mostly by volunteers
  • Registered charities (ACNC), incorporated associations or informal community groups
  • A clear benefit to the community

We take on a small number of free projects at a time, so we can't say yes to everyone. Every application gets a reply.

How it works

Apply, chat, build.

  1. Apply with the form below. It takes about 2 minutes.
  2. We reply within a week to say whether we can help.
  3. A short call to agree what to build.
  4. We build it in between paid projects, so it can take longer than usual.

What's free: our design and build work. Third-party costs, such as a domain name, stay with your organisation, and we'll help you find free non-profit plans where they exist.
